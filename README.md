# Lined

Lined is a handwritten note-taking app for iPad with AI at your fingertips.
You write, annotate and study with Apple Pencil, and the AI is part of the page instead of a chat window next to it: circle anything you don't understand and Lined explains it right there.

Lined is in early development and not available yet.

## What works today

- A library with nested folders and notebook covers. Drag notebooks and folders into folders, or select several to move or delete them at once.
- A4 notebooks on blank, lined, squared or dotted paper, and notebooks made from imported PDFs.
- PDFs shared from other apps through the share sheet, as a new notebook or added to the open one.
- Pages added after the current one or at the end of the page overview, on any paper or from a PDF, and rearranged by dragging them in the overview.
- Handwriting with Apple Pencil on every page, sharp at any zoom, saved as you write.
- A fineliner pen, an eraser and a lasso in a tool bar that docks to any edge of the screen.

## Planned features

- Handwritten notes, PDFs, pages and tabs, built for Apple Pencil.
- Pencil gestures for the AI, such as circling a passage to have it explained.
- An AI that knows what you are learning, opens the documents and pages where you should continue, and answers questions with a quick shortcut or by voice.

## Requirements

- An iPad with Apple Pencil.
- A ChatGPT plan. Lined uses Sign in with ChatGPT, so the AI runs on your own subscription and there is no separate AI fee.

## Development

Lined is a native iPadOS app written in Swift. See [docs/development.md](docs/development.md) to build it.
